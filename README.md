## What it is

A web app that turns a person's photo and a caption into cover art in the
multi-panel GTA 6 poster style — Vice City sunset / neon colour grade — and
exports it as a PNG to share. It runs offline in a desktop browser from its
own static files: the photos never leave the machine.

## The signature mechanic: VICE GRID

The poster is a remixable multi-panel grid. Drag a panel onto another to swap
the photos between them, drag inside a panel (Pan mode) to reposition its
photo, scroll over a panel to zoom it. A grade slider sweeps the whole poster
from golden hour to neon night, live. Three one-tap looks — Sunset Strip,
Neon Night, Heat Wave — re-skin the layout, lettering and colours.

## Features

- Photo upload (file picker or drag-and-drop) plus two built-in sample photos
- Caption and sub-caption editing with heavy outline poster lettering
- Four panel layouts: Triple, Four Corners, Boulevard Strip, Hero + Duo
- Crop and position controls per panel (pan, zoom, reset)
- Export as PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080
- Fully offline: no network requests, no external fonts or libraries

## Try it

Serve `app/` with any static server and open `index.html` in desktop Chrome.
